# Personal workspace

Kekkai brings personal planning and a curated view of external content into one workspace.

## Language

**Task**:
A piece of work that can be completed and may have a due date.
_Avoid_: To-do item

**Deadline**:
A dated commitment displayed on the calendar that can be completed.
_Avoid_: DDL, meeting, event

**Feed source**:
An external publication or service supplying a collection of entries, such as GitHub Trending.

**Feed entry**:
A piece of content shown with enough information to evaluate it before opening its original source.

**Saved entry**:
A feed entry kept for later reading, independently of whether it remains in the current feed.
